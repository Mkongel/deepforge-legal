Deepforge Privacy Policy

Effective 4 November 2026 (updated the same day: cloud save with Google Play Games). "Deepforge" is a working title.

In short: your game progress is saved on your own device and, if you sign in to Google Play Games, also in your Google account (cloud save). We (the developer) don't run any servers ourselves. The game shows optional ads from Google AdMob, sells optional items through Google Play and counts how the game is played with Google Analytics for Firebase, without your name or email. Google collects some data for those, as described below. In the EU, the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time.

Who we are

Deepforge is a mobile game made by an individual developer in Sweden, publishing as "Mkongel" on Google Play ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the data described here. Questions about this policy or your data: see Contact.

What stays on your device

  • Your game progress (items, gold, gear, gems, items you bought, settings and similar) is saved in a file in the game's private storage on your device. It is never sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes this copy. With cloud save on, a copy is also kept in your Google account (see below).
  • Notifications. If you allow them, the game plans reminders on your phone (for example when a forge job is done). They are created and shown on the device; nothing about them is sent anywhere. You can turn them off in your phone's settings.
  • The game has no accounts of its own and no crash reporting, and we don't sell personal data.

Cloud save (Google Play Games)

So that your progress and the items you bought are not lost when you change or reset your phone, the game can keep a copy of your game progress in your Google account with Google Play Games Services ("Saved Games"). This only happens when you are signed in to Google Play Games on the phone (Play Games may sign you in by itself if you use it for other games; you can also sign in from the game's info screen). Without signing in, the game works the same, and your progress stays only on your device.

  • What is stored: the same game progress file as on your device (items, gold, gear, gems, items you bought with their order references, settings), plus a short description (your level) and the time it was saved. It contains no name, email or payment details.
  • Who can see it: it is stored by Google in your Google account, and only Deepforge, signed in as you, can read it. We (the developer) cannot see it.
  • Your Play Games profile: when you sign in, Google Play Games shares your Play Games gamer name and avatar with the game, which shows the name on the info screen. Google also collects analytics and diagnostic data about its own software. This is processed by Google under Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Deleting it: you can delete the game's data in your Play Games profile or your Google account settings, or delete your Play Games profile altogether.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows rewarded ads only when you choose to watch one, for example to double a reward. The ads come from Google AdMob. When the game loads or shows an ad, Google receives and processes data from your device, such as:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address (from which an approximate location can be estimated),
  • information about your device and the app (model, operating system, language, app version) and how you interact with the ads,
  • diagnostic data, such as crash and performance data of the ads software.

Google uses this to show and measure ads, to prevent fraud and, where you allow it, to personalise ads. Google processes it under Google's Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services. We don't receive this data ourselves; AdMob only gives us totals (such as how many ads were shown).

Your consent. In the EU, the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the game shows Google's consent form when you first start it, and asks before ads use your data or personalise ads. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time under Privacy options in the game's info screen (the cog button). Without consent you may still see ads, but they are not personalised. Outside those regions you can limit personalised ads in your phone's settings (for Android: Settings → Google → Ads, where you can also delete your advertising ID).

Ads in Deepforge are set to suit players aged 13 and up.

Play statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To see how the game is played and where it needs to get better, the game sends usage events to Google Analytics for Firebase, for example: that a session started and how long it lasted, which optional bonuses were used and whether an ad, a skip token or the pass paid for them, what was bought in the shop, and progress points such as the level reached or a gate built. These events are linked to a random ID that Firebase creates for the app on your device (the app instance ID), not to your name, email or Google account. Google also receives your IP address (used to estimate a country and region, not stored by us) and information about your device and the app (model, operating system, language, app version).

We see this data only as totals and charts in the Firebase console (for example how many players come back after a week). It is processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and Google's Firebase privacy information. We don't use it to personalise ads.

Your consent. In the EU, the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the statistics are covered by the same consent form as the ads: without your consent, nothing is stored on your device for them and Google receives only limited, cookieless signals. You can change your choice under Privacy options in the info screen.

Purchases (Google Play)

The game is free to play and offers optional in‑app purchases (such as gems, packs and a weekly pass). Payments are made through Google Play: Google handles your payment details, and we never see them. To deliver what you bought, the game receives a confirmation of the purchase from Google (what was bought and an order reference), and the item is stored in your game progress on your device. Google Play shows us order information, such as the order reference, the product and the country, for accounting, refunds and support; it is processed under Google's Privacy Policy. Refunds are handled through Google Play.

Google Play

Google Play handles downloads, updates and, if you join a test, test sign‑up. Google processes that data under Google's Privacy Policy.

Testers

If you join a closed test of Deepforge, we see the email address you were invited with in the Google Play Console. We use it only to run the test and to talk with you about it, and we remove it from our tester list when you ask or when the test no longer needs it. Feedback you send us (for example by email) is used only to improve the game.

Legal bases

Ads use your device's data for personalised ads, and store or read information on your device, only with your consent where the law requires it (EU, EEA, UK, Switzerland); you can withdraw it at any time as described above. Play statistics are processed for our legitimate interest in understanding and improving the game, and in those regions only to the extent your consent choice allows. Purchase information is processed to deliver what you bought and to meet our accounting duties. Cloud save is processed to provide the feature you chose by signing in to Google Play Games. Tester email addresses and feedback are processed because you chose to join the test or write to us.

Children

Deepforge is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

How long data is kept

Game progress stays on your device until you delete it, and the cloud copy stays in your Google account until you delete it (see Cloud save). Purchase records are kept as long as Swedish accounting rules require. Tester emails and feedback are deleted when the test no longer needs them or when you ask. Play statistics events are kept by Google Analytics for 2 months; only totals (such as daily player counts) stay longer. Google keeps the data it collects for ads and payments according to its own policies.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation you can ask what personal data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Because the game keeps your progress on your device and, with cloud save, in your own Google account (which we cannot read), the data we can hold is limited to purchase records from Google Play, a tester email address, feedback you sent us and the play statistics above (which we can't link to you, since they carry no name or email). For data that Google collects for ads, statistics, payments and cloud save, you can also use your Google account settings or contact Google. You can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).
