Deepforge Privacy Policy

Effective 20 October 2026. "Deepforge" is a working title.

In short: the current version of Deepforge does not collect, send or share any personal data. Your game progress stays on your own device.

Who we are

Deepforge is a mobile game made by an individual developer in Sweden, publishing as "Mkongel" on Google Play ("we", "us"). Questions about this policy or your data: see Contact.

What the game stores

  • Your game progress (items, gold, gear, settings and similar) is saved in a file in the game's private storage on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.
  • The game has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no in‑app purchases in this version, and it does not use the internet.

Google Play

Google Play handles downloads, updates and, if you join a test, test sign‑up. Google processes that data under Google's Privacy Policy.

Testers

If you join a closed test of Deepforge, we see the email address you were invited with in the Google Play Console. We use it only to run the test and to talk with you about it, and we remove it from our tester list when you ask or when the test no longer needs it. Feedback you send us (for example by email) is used only to improve the game.

Children

Deepforge is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Planned features

Later versions may add advertising (Google AdMob), in‑app purchases (Google Play Billing) and cloud saves (Google Play Games). These involve data handled by Google, such as an advertising ID or your Google account. Before a version with any of them is released, this policy will be updated to describe exactly what is collected and why, and the game will ask for your consent where the law requires it.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation you can ask what personal data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Today the only data we can hold is a tester email address or feedback you sent us. You can also complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).

Changes to this policy
